## Intro:

Algebra is a fundamental branch of mathematics that plays a crucial role in engineering problem-solving. It provides the tools and techniques necessary for modeling, analyzing, and solving engineering problems involving unknown quantities, relationships, and systems. This page introduces key algebraic concepts and their applications in engineering contexts.
