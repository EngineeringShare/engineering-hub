About the simulation

DC resistors, LEDs, diodes, ideal switches, copper, wire links and meters. Open switches disconnect their leads; closed switches have zero resistance. Ammeters close a circuit in series; voltmeters draw no current. Ideal parallel conductors can have indeterminate current splits. LEDs and diodes use approximate forward thresholds with 20 Ω and 1 Ω slope resistance respectively; reverse current is blocked. Diodes default to 0.7 V. The gate and flip-flop ICs use an HC-style electrical approximation: high-impedance inputs, 2–6 V supply, approximate LOW/HIGH thresholds at 30%/70% of VCC (25%/75% near 2 V), and 50 Ω output drivers. Floating inputs read X; undefined or unpowered outputs are undriven. The 74x90 uses LS-style input thresholds (LOW ≤0.8 V, HIGH ≥2 V) and a 4.75–5.25 V supply; its output drivers are approximate. Flip-flops and counters respond to wired clock edges in Simulate mode and store their state. Reset/preset inputs act immediately in either mode. Power loss clears stored state to X. No propagation delay, setup/hold timing, switch bounce, input clamp diodes, quiescent IC current, reverse breakdown, AC, transient, damage or thermal models. 74HC08 datasheet.
