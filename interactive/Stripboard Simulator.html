About the simulation

DC resistors, LEDs, diodes, ideal switches, copper, wire links and meters. Open switches disconnect their leads; closed switches have zero resistance. Ammeters close a circuit in series; voltmeters draw no current. Ideal parallel conductors can have indeterminate current splits. LEDs and diodes use approximate forward thresholds with 20 Ω and 1 Ω slope resistance respectively; reverse current is blocked. Diodes default to 0.7 V. No reverse breakdown, AC, transient, damage or thermal models.
